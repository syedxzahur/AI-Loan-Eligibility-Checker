About AI Loan Eligibility Checker

Architecture, methodology, and BFSI compliance disclosures.

1. Core Financial Methodology

The platform applies established retail banking underwriting heuristics used across Indian and international BFSI institutions:

  • Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR / DTI): Ensures total monthly loan installments stay within 40% to 55% of verifiable monthly earnings.
  • Net Disposable Income (NDI): Computes household cashflow surplus after living expenses and debt payments to prevent overleveraging.
  • Standard Reducing Balance EMI: Utilizes exponential compounding monthly rate formulas consistent with retail banks.
  • Risk-Adjusted Benchmark Spreads: Maps applicant credit scores to benchmark lending spreads.

2. Privacy & Data Handling Policy

We respect your financial confidentiality. The application adheres to strict data minimization principles:

  • No passwords, banking OTPs, or Aadhaar / PAN numbers are ever requested or stored.
  • Analysis submissions sent to Google Sheets are handled strictly through a secure, credentialed serverless proxy.
  • Zero API keys or service account credentials are ever exposed in client-side code.

3. Official Disclaimer

This web application is an educational decision-support simulator. It does not constitute a loan offer, pre-approval, financial advisory contract, or formal credit sanction. All loan sanctions are at the sole discretion of the respective lending financial institution following physical verification of documentation and statutory underwriting guidelines.
